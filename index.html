06 — Why Now

Four shifts. One moment.

01

The Great Developer Shortage

85 million skilled tech workers will be missing by 2030. Companies can't hire fast enough. The answer isn't more developers — it's making creation accessible to everyone with an idea.

02

AI Has Matured — Finally

For the first time, AI can understand intent and translate it into working systems. Not just generating code snippets, but architecting complete products. The gap between "I have an idea" and "I have a product" has never been smaller.

Physical AI is Real

07 — The Path Forward

Three phases to reshape creation.

Phase I — Foundation

Set the Standard

Build the best-in-class creation tools for both software and hardware individually. Launch the platform. Earn the trust of early creators. Prove that one person with an idea can ship a real product.

Phase II — Convergence

Build the Junction

Unite both halves. A smart home device designed in one session — the app and the hardware together. The firmware and the interface, generated in tandem. One vision, one workflow, one product.

From Screen to Factory

Close the loop with integrated manufacturing. From your screen to a physical product in your hands. The complete journey — sketch to scale — realized. No middlemen. No friction.
